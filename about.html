Our Story

A Taste of Andhra, Right Here in Chennai

Guntur Gongura brings the rich and bold flavours of Andhra cuisine to Chennai, with dishes inspired by traditional South Indian cooking.

From aromatic biryanis and flavourful curries to delicious starters, seafood and traditional favourites, our menu celebrates the variety and character of Andhra food.

Every dish is prepared to create a memorable dining experience while keeping the spirit of Andhra flavours at the heart of the meal.
